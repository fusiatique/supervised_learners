Maze RL Simulation

A Q-learning agent with a dopamine-related state d and accumulated harm h learns which sources to use: food, social interaction, rest or two drugs. A computational hypothesis in abstract units, not a model of physiology.

Explore the maze

The face icon represents the agent, showing its location and current feeling. The plate of pasta bolognese represents food, and the pill represents Drug B. The agent learns from experience as it moves through the maze and chooses which items to use.

Maze RL Simulation

A reinforcement-learning agent explores a maze and learns which item is worth going for.

Setup

Maze Medium (13 × 13)

Items

The Medium (13 × 13) maze starts with an open interior and a fixed outer wall. Pick an item and click a cell to place it. Choose Wall to paint interior walls by dragging, or Eraser to remove walls by dragging and items by clicking.

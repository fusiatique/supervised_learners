Minimal Dependence Model

A Q-learning agent keeps an internal level H near its setpoint. Each drug use lowers the baseline B that H falls back to. Once B is below the tipping point, natural rewards can no longer reach the setpoint and the drug becomes the agent’s best option.

Life events

Let the agent learn natural rewards first (a few thousand steps at high speed), then make the drug available.

A forced dose raises H and lowers B, but the agent did not choose it and learns nothing from it directly.
